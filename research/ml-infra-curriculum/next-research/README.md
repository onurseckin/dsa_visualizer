## Rules

- Do not use 23 topics, 232 questions, or an equal per-topic quota as a target
  constraint.
- Reuse or cross-link existing canonical DSA problems instead of cloning them.
- Treat an online question as a prerequisite only when its operations transfer
  to the named ML algorithm.
- Require a direct, executable ML bridge for every advanced topic.
- Treat documentation, articles, and papers as references rather than online
  judge problems.
- Verify every retained online question with an exact title, platform ID,
  direct URL, and learning purpose.
- Use the agentic protocol’s specialist, cross-review, revision, and fresh
  final-gate loops; a single agent fan-out is not sufficient.
